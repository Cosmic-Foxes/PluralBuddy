## What is PluralBuddy?

Plurality bots have only popped up on Discord within recent years. They are bots that take a message sent by a user, sends a copy of the message as a different identity/character/etc, and then delete the original message in a very short space of time to make it appear as if you are typing as someone, or something, else. They can be needed for numerous reasons, often for making somebody more comfortable chatting within a server, plurality bots increase the positive atmosphere that a server has overall.

PluralBuddy is a plurality bot, designed from the ground up to make setting up and the overall usability as easy as possible. Plurality bots are complex in nature due to their structure and overall commands needed to complete something that needs to be done. PluralBuddy aims to solve this exact issue by making things as straightforward for the user as possible, along with different modes of configuration depending on what is needed.
